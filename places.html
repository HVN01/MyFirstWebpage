Must-Visit Places

Ha Long Bay

Ha Long Bay is famous for its emerald waters and thousands of towering limestone islands topped with rainforests. Visitors can take boat cruises, kayak through hidden lagoons, and explore scenic caves.

Hoi An Ancient Town

Hoi An Ancient Town is known for its well-preserved traditional architecture, riverside markets, and glowing lantern-lit streets that reflect centuries of rich cultural heritage.

Sapa

Sapa is renowned for its lush terraced rice fields, cool highland climate, and panoramic mountain views in northern Vietnam, making it an ideal destination for trekking and cultural discovery.
